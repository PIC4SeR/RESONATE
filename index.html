RESONATE

a Real Social Navigation Testbed for Autonomous Service Robots

Stefano Trepella*, Andrea Ostuni*, Mauro Martini, Irene Borgini, Marcello Chiaberge

Politecnico di Torino · Intesa Sanpaolo Innovation Center · *equal contribution

Paper Dataset Code

Trajectory animations

A Clearpath Jackal navigates past people tracked by Vicon in 8 scenarios. 8 local controllers were tested, with 5 runs each. Pick a scenario and an algorithm, then click a run to play it. Animations play in real time.

RunResultTTG [s]PL [m]Min dist [m]SW/sSAFRNAAF

TTG: time to reach the goal (whole run if never reached). PL: path length. Min dist: minimum distance between robot and person body surfaces (negative = overlap). SW/s: social work per second. SA, FR, NA, AF: mean human ratings (1–5) of safety, friendliness, naturalness and avoidance foresight; – = not rated.

Animations and metrics from the RESONATE dataset, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
